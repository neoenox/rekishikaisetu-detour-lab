# Channel description (paste into YouTube)

Published to the channel on 2026-11-25 with bsk. YouTube allows 1000 characters, so this version is shorter than the original draft.

The Detour Lab checks the things you think you know. We use real sources, maps, charts, and simple math.

Does a world map show countries at the wrong size? Where did a famous story about the past come from? We look at the sources and compare them.

We do not rush to an answer. We separate what we can say from what we cannot say yet, and follow the question left at the end.

What the videos look like
- Voice-over with maps, diagrams, charts, and simple calculations
- No face on camera, no live presenter
- Every number has a source, listed in the description
- If something is not confirmed, we say it is not confirmed
- AI-generated background video, disclosed in the description

Corrections are welcome. If you find a mistake, leave a comment. We will check it and correct the video or the description.
